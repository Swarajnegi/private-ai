## Poem 1 — "A long way from home"

*Posted 2024-10-26, 5:32 PM. Closes by quoting Frost's "Stopping by Woods on a
Snowy Evening" — the last three lines are Frost's, used as the resolution.*

The world's unjust; no one to trust,
But if I stop now—call it a bust.
I'm a long way from home,
Out here to build a new Rome.

Now they want me to stop and grieve,
But I'm still here—not one to flee;
For what is to be done, must be done,
And whatever is to come, may come.

It's been so long since I've grieved;
I think I've forgotten how to bleed.
For now I know why rest feels steep;
I guess I'm just unable, to weep.

The woods are lovely, dark, and deep,
But I have promises to keep—
And miles to go before I sleep,
And miles to go before I sleep.

## Poem 2 — "I just don't want her, anymore"

I tried the best I can
Guess I was dealt a losing hand
You've been loved before; now you're through
But not like this, that much is true

I bear the disdain of endless tries
I guess I've learned that love can die.
But she can see I'm not fine,
I guess they lied—love isn't blind.

But look what you've done, my dear,
Look what I've become.
I write in verses, drowning in curses
I'll leave now, before it worsens.

There must be a reason, for it's the law.
My feeling's raw, or perhaps I'm the flaw
For each word ignites a silent war
I lose, but that's not all.

She won't give me what I need—
The love I heed, the care I seek
Won't respect the worth of my deeds
But now I dream of being freed

Breaking the silence, tearing the veil
No longer captive to this fragile tale
Yes it's my fault my heart's sore

But I just don't want her, anymore.
